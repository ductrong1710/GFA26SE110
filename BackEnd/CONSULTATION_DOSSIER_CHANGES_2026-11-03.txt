Truoc khi sua:

- Chua bat buoc bill coc.
- BE yeu cau Admin lien ket khach/vuon truoc khi ky su gui.

Sau khi sua:
1. Khach gui form Homepage. Nguoi truc lien he ben ngoai.
2. Ky su nong nghiep tu van, chot thiet bi va nhan coc ben ngoai he thong.
3. Ky su nhap thong tin vuon, thiet bi, so luong, thoi han va lich mong muon.
4. Ky su nhap ho ten/email khach neu chua lien ket, dinh kem bill va gui mot lan.
5. BE luu khach/vuon/quyen chu vuon trong cung transaction voi ho so.
   Khach/vuon da lien ket hop le duoc giu nguyen; khong tao lai khi gui lai.
   Tai khoan moi chi la FarmOwner, dung mat khau ngau nhien khong tiet lo;
   khach co the dung chuc nang quen mat khau de dat mat khau sau nay.
6. Admin nhan thong bao, thay ho so cho duyet tren dashboard/Lap dat & dich vu.
7. Admin xem thong tin khach, vuon, gia da chot va anh bill; tra bo sung hoac duyet.
8. Duyet tao dung mot yeu cau APPROVED; chuyen sang man hinh phan cong KTV.
   Admin khong phai nhap lai khach hang hay tao lai vuon.
9. Cac buoc cap phat, lap dat, ky giay, hau kiem va thue ngay hom sau giu nguyen.
